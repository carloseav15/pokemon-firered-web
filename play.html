FireRed controls

Keyboard

  • Arrow keys or W A S DMove through maps and menus
  • ZA button (Talk, interact, confirm)
  • XB button (Cancel or go back; hold to run)
  • EnterStart (Open the game menu)
  • SpaceSelect (Use the registered item)
  • Q / EL/R buttons
  • MToggle sound

Phone or tablet

Use the on-screen directional pad and A, B, Start and Select buttons. Toggle them in the toolbar.

Display modes

  • 🎮 GBA Console (3:2): Display the game inside the GBA frame.
  • 📺 Fit to Window: Automatically fits the browser window.
  • 🗺️ World Map: Open the regional world map.
